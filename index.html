QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Do I need an app?

No. LoveTree is designed around a mobile web setup, so you can connect and control it from your phone without installing an app.

Does it show social media posts?

No. LoveTree is an ambient display. It turns recent activity levels into light and movement rather than showing usernames, messages, or a social feed.

Does every online post make it flash?

No. The activity is intentionally smoothed over time so the tree feels like it is breathing and swelling, not firing off notifications.

Can I control the brightness?

Yes. The product experience is designed to include brightness controls and quiet hours.
